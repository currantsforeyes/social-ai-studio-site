Privacy Policy

How the local-first desktop application handles workspace data and optional connected-account data.

Effective date: 11 August 2026 / Last updated: 14 August 2026

Social AI Studio is designed to keep your working files and planning data on your computer. This policy explains what the application stores locally, what happens when you connect an external service, and the choices available to you.

1. Local-first workspace data

The application stores brands, campaigns, post drafts, platform variants, media references, workflow metadata, settings, approval decisions and local planning history on your computer. This information is used to provide the desktop workspace and is not sent to Social AI Studio by default.

Media files and ComfyUI workflows remain part of your local working environment. When you use a local ComfyUI installation, generation requests and results are handled by that local service according to its own configuration.

2. Optional TikTok account data

You choose whether to connect a TikTok account. The connection begins from the desktop application and uses TikTok's permission flow. Social AI Studio uses only the permissions you approve for the account actions supported by the current integration.

Access and refresh tokens for an authorised connection are stored locally using the application's protected token storage. They are not sold or shared by Social AI Studio. External content publishing is currently disabled while platform approval and the required publishing access are completed.

3. External services

If you deliberately connect a third-party service, that service may process information under its own privacy policy and terms. You remain responsible for reviewing the permissions shown during account connection and for the content you choose to send to that service.

4. Your choices and retention

  • You can stop using an integration and remove its local account profile from the application.
  • You can revoke platform permissions through the connected platform.
  • You can delete local workspace data and media files using the controls provided by your operating system and the application.
  • Scheduled work remains local planning data; the application does not publish content automatically.

5. Security

Keep your computer, operating system account and local application data protected. Do not share account tokens, passwords or private workflow files in support requests. No software can guarantee absolute security, but Social AI Studio is designed to minimise unnecessary transfer of your working data.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or the desktop application, open a support issue on GitHub. Include the app version and a short description, but never include tokens or passwords.

This policy may be updated when the application's integrations or data handling change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
