LOCAL CREATION

Bring your own creative tools.

Social AI Studio works alongside a local ComfyUI installation. Import workflows in API format, map prompts and inputs, launch compatible image generation or editing jobs, and transfer finished results into your media library.

  • Workflow-aware prompt, seed, sampler and size controls
  • Reference image and mask support when the workflow provides a real loader input
  • Generated asset metadata for workflow, prompt, seed and source image

CONTROL BY DESIGN

Approval is a step, not a suggestion.

Every post can move through an explicit local state. Decisions, notes and rejection reasons remain attached to the work so you can understand what changed and why.

  • Draft, pending approval, approved, rejected and scheduled states
  • Approval invalidation when a platform variant changes
  • Queue blockers for missing approval or disconnected accounts

TIKTOK INTEGRATION

Connect only when you choose to.

Social AI Studio uses TikTok Login Kit for an optional account connection. A user starts the connection from the desktop app, reviews the platform permission flow and can revoke the local connection from settings.

At this stage, external content publishing is not enabled. Local planning, review and scheduling remain available while platform approval and the required publishing access are completed.

YOUR WORKSPACE, YOUR FILES

Local-first by default.

SUPPORT AND FAQ

Clear answers before you install.

We are building Social AI Studio in public, with the product surface and its current limitations documented plainly.

Is Social AI Studio a web app?

No. It is a local-first desktop application. This website is the official product and service information site.

Does the app publish to TikTok automatically?

No. The app does not publish content automatically. TikTok connection is optional and external publishing is currently disabled pending platform approval.

What happens to my content?

Your local working data and media references stay on your computer by default. See the Privacy Policy for the current account-connection details.

Where can I get help?

For product or integration questions, open a support issue on GitHub. Include the app version and a short description of the issue; never include account tokens or passwords.
